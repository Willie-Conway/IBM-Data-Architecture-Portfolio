**3. Areas for Improvement**

| Sr. No.     | Area for Improvement                                            | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Impact                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1** | **Immature Data Governance Framework**                    | As a startup-origin company, TrendyThreads has prioritized speed of innovation and feature development over establishing formal data governance. While governance tools like Collibra and Alation are mentioned in the architecture, actual governance policies, data ownership structures, and stewardship programs may not be fully mature or consistently enforced across the organization. | • Risk of data quality issues compounding as data volumes grow exponentially`<br>`• Potential compliance gaps with regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) leading to fines and reputational damage`<br>`• Difficulty in maintaining data lineage and audit trails for regulatory reporting`<br>`• Inconsistent data definitions and metrics across teams causing reporting conflicts`<br>`• Challenges in scaling governance processes as the company grows and adds more data sources`<br>`• May complicate integration with more mature organizations like FashionMart that have established governance expectations`<br>`• Data access and security policies may not be uniformly applied |
| **2** | **Complex Technology Stack**                              | The architecture includes a wide array of specialized tools and platforms: multiple streaming technologies, processing frameworks, databases, analytics tools, and ML platforms. While each tool is best-in-class for its purpose, the overall stack complexity is high and requires diverse, specialized expertise to maintain and operate effectively.                                       | • Higher operational complexity and maintenance costs due to multiple systems`<br>`• Requires broad and deep skill sets across the data team, making hiring challenging`<br>`• Potential integration challenges and data consistency issues between components`<br>`• Increased attack surface for security vulnerabilities across many systems`<br>`• May slow down development and feature delivery due to integration complexity`<br>`• Difficult to troubleshoot issues that span across the technology stack`<br>`• Higher cognitive load for team members who must understand multiple systems`<br>`• Vendor lock-in risks with specialized platforms                       |
| **3** | **Potential Logical Data Silos from Specialized Systems** | While the architecture avoids physical silos, the use of specialized systems for different use cases (CRM for customer data, PIM for product data, data warehouse for analytics, real-time store for operational data, cloud storage for media) can create logical data silos if not properly integrated with a unified data fabric or semantic layer.                                         | • Data may be duplicated across systems leading to inconsistencies`<br>`• Complex data lineage tracking across multiple platforms`<br>`• Challenges in creating a truly unified 360-degree customer view`<br>`• Potential for inconsistent reporting if data from different sources doesn't align`<br>`• Increased ETL/ELT complexity to consolidate data for cross-functional analysis`<br>`• Business users may struggle to access data spanning multiple systems`<br>`• Data governance becomes more challenging with distributed data ownership`<br>`• Real-time synchronization requirements add additional complexity                                                       |
